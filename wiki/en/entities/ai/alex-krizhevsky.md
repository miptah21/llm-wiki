# Alex Krizhevsky

**Alex Krizhevsky** is a computer scientist best known for his work in deep learning and computer vision.

He is the primary author of the seminal paper [[source-Krizhevsky-2012]], which introduced **AlexNet**, a deep convolutional neural network that won the ImageNet ILSVRC-2012 competition. This work is widely credited with accelerating the deep learning revolution in the 2010s.

During his PhD at the University of Toronto, supervised by [[geoffrey-hinton]], he developed the `cuda-convnet` library, a highly optimized GPU implementation of 2D convolutions that made training large neural networks computationally feasible.\n
