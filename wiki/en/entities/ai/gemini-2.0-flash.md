# Gemini 2.0 Flash

**Gemini 2.0 Flash** is a large language model by Google (version `gemini-2.0-flash-001`). It is part of the Gemini model family designed for fast, cost-efficient inference with extended context capabilities.

## Role in Cheat-Sheet ICL Research

- Used to evaluate **transferability** of cheat sheets: cheat sheets created by [[gpt-4.1]] were directly applied to Gemini 2.0 Flash.
- Results showed good cross-model transferability in most tasks.
- Exceptions occurred only on tasks where Gemini 2.0 Flash itself showed no many-shot gains (highlighted with gray background in the paper's Figure 3).
