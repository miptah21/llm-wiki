# GPT-4.1

**GPT-4.1** is a large language model by OpenAI (version `gpt-4.1-2025-04-14`). It supports extended context windows and was used as the primary model in Honda et al. (2025) for rationale augmentation, cheat-sheet creation, and inference evaluation.

## Role in Cheat-Sheet ICL Research

- **Primary evaluation model:** All main results in the paper use GPT-4.1.
- **Cheat-sheet creator:** Cheat sheets generated by GPT-4.1 transfer effectively to other models (e.g., [[gemini-2.0-flash]]).
- Selected over `gemini-1.5-pro-001` because it is a stronger many-shot learner on BBH tasks, offers better performance-cost trade-off, and is currently available (unlike the deprecated Gemini version).
