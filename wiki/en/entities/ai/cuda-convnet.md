# cuda-convnet

**cuda-convnet** is a highly optimized C++/CUDA implementation of convolutional neural networks.

Developed by [[alex-krizhevsky]] during his PhD studies, the library was specifically written to leverage NVIDIA GPUs for high-speed 2D convolutions and GPU parallelization. It was open-sourced and served as the computational engine for training AlexNet in [[source-Krizhevsky-2012]], laying the groundwork for subsequent deep learning frameworks like Caffe, TensorFlow, and PyTorch.\n
