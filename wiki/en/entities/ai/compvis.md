# CompVis

**CompVis** (Computer Vision and Learning Group) is a research group based at the Ludwig Maximilian University of Munich (LMU Munich), Germany, led by Professor Björn Ommer. The group specializes in computer vision, deep learning, and generative AI research.

## Major Achievements

CompVis is internationally recognized for developing:
- **VQGAN** (Vector Quantized GAN): A model that combines transformer architectures with adversarial training to generate high-resolution images (Esser et al., 2021).
- **Latent Diffusion Models (LDMs)**: The underlying framework that represents the core technology of **Stable Diffusion** (Rombach et al., 2022).
- **Stable Diffusion Release**: Collaborative development and open-weight release of Stable Diffusion in partnership with Runway ML, Stability AI, and LAION.
