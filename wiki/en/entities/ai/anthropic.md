# Anthropic

**Anthropic** is an American artificial intelligence (AI) safety and research company. Founded in 2021 by former members of OpenAI, including siblings Daniela Amodei and Dario Amodei, the company specializes in developing large language models (LLMs) and advanced AI systems with a strong emphasis on safety, steerability, and alignment.

Anthropic is the creator of the **Claude** family of language models, as well as developer-focused tools such as **[[claude-code]]**.

## Key Products & Technologies

- **Claude**: A state-of-the-art family of large language models (including Claude 3, Claude 3.5, and Claude 4) renowned for high cognitive capabilities, long context windows, and advanced reasoning.
- **Claude Code**: An agentic command-line assistant ([[claude-code]]) designed to run directly in the developer terminal.
- **Constitutional AI**: A pioneering alignment methodology developed by Anthropic that trains AI systems to adhere to a set of principles or a "constitution," reducing harmful outputs without relying solely on manual human feedback.
