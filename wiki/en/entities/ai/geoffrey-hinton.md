# Geoffrey Hinton

**Geoffrey Hinton** is a cognitive psychologist and computer scientist, often referred to as the "Godfather of AI".

A professor at the University of Toronto, Hinton has been a pivotal figure in the deep learning community. He co-authored [[source-Krizhevsky-2012]] (AlexNet) with his students [[alex-krizhevsky]] and [[ilya-sutskever]]. Hinton was awarded the Turing Award in 2018 (alongside Yoshua Bengio and Yann LeCun) and the Nobel Prize in Physics in 2024 for his foundational contributions to artificial neural networks.\n
