# Ilya Sutskever

**Ilya Sutskever** is a leading computer scientist in the field of artificial intelligence and deep learning.

He co-authored [[source-Krizhevsky-2012]] (AlexNet) during his time at the University of Toronto under [[geoffrey-hinton]]. He later co-founded OpenAI, where he served as Chief Scientist and played a critical role in the development of GPT-series models and other groundbreaking generative AI technologies.\n
