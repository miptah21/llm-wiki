# Vannevar Bush

**Vannevar Bush** (1890–1974) was an American engineer, inventor, and science administrator. He is widely recognized as a pioneer of the information age, most notably for his influential 1945 essay *As We May Think*, in which he proposed the conceptual [[memex]] device—a precursor to hypertext and modern web navigation. His vision of a private, actively curated knowledge store with associative trails laid the conceptual foundation for the [[llm-wiki-pattern]].
