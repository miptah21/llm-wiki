# Memex

The **Memex** (a portmanteau of "memory index") is a hypothetical analog computer proposed by [[vannevar-bush]] in his 1945 article *As We May Think*. Bush envisioned the Memex as an interactive device that individuals could use to store all of their books, records, and communications, and link them together using "associative trails." It served as a major inspiration for early hypertext research and represents the original conceptual ancestor of personal compounding vaults like the [[llm-wiki-pattern]].
